    0. Briefing¶

    0.1 What you are building¶

    Pickleball tournaments are planned in the Tournament Calculator (a web page). Its plan is then turned into a Google Sheets workbook by the Standard Tournament Generator (an Apps Script). A category (for example "Novice Mixed Doubles") is split into brackets. Each bracket plays a round robin (every pair plays every other pair). The best pairs then play a playoff.

    When a category has exactly one bracket, the tools offer two playoff formats today:

    • ttb: twice-to-beat. RR #1 v RR #2 for gold, where #1 needs one win and #2 must win twice. RR #3 takes bronze without playing.
    • rr: no playoff. The round-robin standings decide gold, silver and bronze.

    You add three things. The first two apply to one-bracket categories only.

    1. A double round robin (rr_legs: 2): every pairing plays twice, as a "leg 1" and a "leg 2".
    2. Two more one-bracket playoff formats (solo_format):
    3. final_bronze: RR #1 v RR #2, one game, for gold. RR #3 v RR #4, one game, for bronze. Needs 4 or more pairs.
    4. final: RR #1 v RR #2, one game, for gold. RR #3 takes bronze without playing.

    Any leg count goes with any of the four formats, which makes eight combinations:

    rr_legs final_bronze final ttb rr
    1 (single, default) new new exists exists
    2 (double) new new new new

    The organiser who asked for this wants two of them: single + final_bronze ("Format A") and double + final ("Format B"). They also want to be able to choose a bronze match or twice-to-beat after a double round robin, so all eight are built.

    1. Best-of-3 round-robin meetings (rr_best_of: 3), in a category with any number of brackets ("Format C"). Every pairing meets once, and each meeting is a best-of-3 series: the first pair to win two games takes it, so game 3 is played only at 1–1. The playoff is the one that category would have anyway, and every playoff match keeps its usual length: the multi-bracket ladder (QF, SF, Final, Bronze) is one game a match, and a one-bracket category still chooses ttb, final_bronze, final or rr. In the standings, W and L count meetings won and lost, not games. The quotient counts the points of every game played.

    Best of 3 does not combine with a double round robin: rr_best_of: 3 and rr_legs: 2 together are rejected. A 2-pair category already plays a best-of-3 Final and has no round robin, so best of 3 does not apply to it.

    Items 1 and 2 need no change on the site and none in the sync API. Item 3 does: today, only a series Final (<KEY>_F_<seat>_(<game>)) knows that a game is not needed. To count a best-of-3 meeting, the site's lib/v1/domain/ and the API's facilityCompletion.mjs learn a second kind of series (§5, §6).

    2. The model¶

    2.1 Round-robin legs¶

    rr_legs is 1 (default) or 2, per category. It is honoured only when the category resolves to one bracket of 3 or more pairs. With L legs and a bracket of n pairs:

    round-robin matches  = L × n(n − 1) / 2
round-robin rounds   = L × (n odd ? n : n − 1)      (the minimum number of time slots)

    The playoff is seeded from the final standings exactly as with a single round robin.

    2.2 The four one-bracket formats¶

    solo_format Playoff matches (max / min) Pairs seated from the round robin Bronze Needs
    ttb (default) 2 / 1 2 RR #3, no match 3+ pairs
    rr 0 / 0 0 from standings 3+ pairs
    final_bronze 2 / 2 4 played: RR #3 v RR #4 4+ pairs
    final 1 / 1 2 RR #3, no match 3+ pairs

    2.3 Time slots for the playoff¶

    solo_format Playoff slots
    ttb 2 (the games run one after the other)
    rr 0
    final_bronze ceil(2 / courts). Final and Bronze have four different pairs, so they share a slot on 2+ courts
    final 1

    2.4 Worked examples (calculator verification targets)¶

    One category. Settings: 2 courts, 20-minute matches, 0-minute buffer, start 08:00.

    Category Pairs Legs Format RR matches Playoff Total Slots Finish
    A1 4 1 final_bronze 6 2 8 4 9:20 AM
    A2 5 1 final_bronze 10 2 12 6 10:00 AM
    B1 3 2 final 6 1 7 7 10:20 AM
    B2 4 2 final 12 1 13 7 10:20 AM
    B3 4 2 final_bronze 12 2 14 7 10:20 AM
    B4 3 2 ttb 6 2 (min 1) 8 (7–8) 8 10:40 AM

    The working, using the calculator's own rule (calcAll: slots = max(ceil(total / courts), RR slots + playoff slots), where RR slots = max(ceil(RR matches / courts), round-robin rounds)):

    • A1: RR slots max(3, 3) = 3, playoff 1, slots max(4, 4) = 4, 4 × 20 min = 80 min.
    • B1: RR slots max(3, 6) = 6, playoff 1, slots max(4, 7) = 7, 140 min. In a 3-pair bracket one pair sits out every round, so the second court is idle.

    2.5 Best-of-3 meetings¶

    rr_best_of is 1 (default) or 3, per category. It is honoured in any standard category with a round robin (3 or more pairs, any number of brackets), and only with rr_legs 1. With a bracket of n pairs:

    meetings             = n(n − 1) / 2
round-robin games    = 3 × meetings          (the plan, at full length)
least games          = 2 × meetings          (every meeting won 2–0)
round-robin rounds   = 3 × (n odd ? n : n − 1)
    • A meeting's three games run back to back on one court. A round of the round robin therefore takes three time slots, and the plan counts every game 3. Twice-to-beat is already planned at its full length in the same way. When a meeting ends 2–0, its game 3 is not played, the court is free a slot early, and the day ends earlier than planned.
    • Standings. W is the number of meetings a pair won (2–0 or 2–1) and L the number it lost. A tied game counts for neither pair, and a meeting that never reaches two wins counts for neither. Points scored, points conceded and the quotient add up every game played, as they do today. The site ranks by W, then head-to-head meetings won among the tied pairs, then quotient. The category tab ranks nothing itself.
    • The playoff is seeded from those standings exactly as today, and plays exactly as it would without best of 3. Each playoff match is one game, except the twice-to-beat Final's two games.
    • Codes carry no game. Each pair keeps its one code <KEY>_<i>. The three games of a meeting are three rows with the same two codes, and match number puts them in order (§4.9).

    The same settings as §2.4: 2 courts, 20-minute matches, 0-minute buffer, start 08:00. Each row is one category with rr_best_of 3.

    Category Pairs Brackets Playoff Meetings RR games (least) Playoff matches Total (least) Slots Finish
    C1 4 1 final_bronze 6 18 (12) 2 20 (14) 10 11:20 AM
    C2 4 1 ttb 6 18 (12) 2 (min 1) 20 (13) 11 11:40 AM
    C3 8 2, top 2 advance SF, Bronze, Final 12 36 (24) 4 40 (28) 20 2:40 PM

    The working, by calcAll's rule as in §2.4:

    • C3: RR slots max(ceil(36 / 2), 3 × 3) = 18. The playoff is the semifinals (1 slot), then the Final and Bronze together (1 slot), so 2 slots. Slots max(ceil(40 / 2), 20) = 20, 400 min. With single games the same category is 16 matches in 8 slots, finishing at 10:40 AM, as the calculator shows today.
    • C4: RR slots max(ceil(9 / 2), 3 × 3) = 9. One pair sits out each round, so the second court is idle throughout.

    3.3 calcCategory(c, cfg)¶

    After sizes and groups are computed, and before playoffPlan is called:

  solo='final';
  notes.push('Final + bronze needs 4 pairs, so RR #3 takes bronze without a match.');
}
const games = (c.rrBestOf===3 && groups>0) ? 3 : 1;
const legs = (c.rrLegs===2 && groups===1 && games===1) ? 2 : 1;
if(c.rrLegs===2 && games===3) notes.push('Best-of-3 meetings are played once per pairing, so this category is not a double round robin.');
else if(c.rrLegs===2 && groups>1) notes.push('A double round robin is for one bracket only, so this category plays a single round robin.');

    • Count meetings, then games: const meetings = rrMatches(sizes) * legs; and const rr = meetings * games;. rr stays the number of round-robin matches (rows in SCHEDULE), which is what every timing function reads.
    • totalMin becomes meetings * (games===3 ? 2 : 1) + plan.min. total stays rr + plan.total.
    • Pass solo (not c.soloFormat) to playoffPlan.
    • Add legs, games, meetings and solo to the returned object.
    • The teams === 2 best-of-3 early return is unchanged. It has no round robin, so legs and games are ignored there. Give its returned object legs:1, games:1, meetings:0 so no reader meets an undefined.

    10. Not in scope¶

    • Labelling the second meeting "Leg 2" where players see it. Only the MATCHES tab labels it.
    • A double round robin for 2+ brackets, in a dual meet or in a team event.
    • Seating RR #1–#4 automatically. The operator types the names, as for twice-to-beat today.
    • Any change to the existing ttb, rr, best-of-3 or multi-bracket playoffs.
    • A best-of-3 final after a round robin. This was discussed and set aside: the site's series rule already handles three games, so it would be a fifth one-bracket format in the calculator and generator only.
    • Interleaving the legs. All of leg 1 is played before any of leg 2:
    • halfway through, the standings mean something
    • a day running late can drop leg 2 and still have a complete round robin
    • no pairing plays twice in a row.

    The generator's suggested packing already keeps that order, because the leg-2 matches queue after leg 1. The operator keeps it when packing SCHEDULE by hand. - Labelling a best-of-3 game "Game 2 of 3" where players see it (D12). Only the MATCHES tab labels it, and a game 3 that is not needed is greyed, as in a series Final today. - Best-of-3 meetings in a dual meet or a team event, or together with a double round robin (D10). - Longer playoff matches after a best-of-3 round robin. The playoff plays as it would without best of 3 (D8). - Spreading a meeting's games across the day. They run back to back on one court (§2.5).

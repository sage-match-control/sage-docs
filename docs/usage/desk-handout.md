# Desk handout

*Send this with your desk link. It is written to read on a phone.*

You mark people in as they arrive. A person is marked in **once**, and that covers every
category they play that day at your venue. You can't do anything else with this link.

## Open it

Open the **link** the operator sent you (or scan its QR code). It works for **one day**,
until the end of that day in Manila time.

If it shows *Ask the operator for a new desk link*, the link has stopped. Ask the operator.

## Mark someone in

1. **Pick your venue** at the top, if the day has more than one.
2. **Find the person.** Type in the search box (a name, a team code or an exact shirt
   size), tap a **category** or **team** chip to show only that one, or use **Jump to…** to
   scroll to a section while still seeing everyone.
3. **Flip their switch.** It shows *Saving…* for a moment, then *In 8:14 AM*.

A person who plays in two categories is **one switch**, shown in both places. When
everyone on a team is in, the team shows **Ready**.

## If you flip the wrong person

Flip the switch back. That clears the time.

## Seeing other desks' marks

Marks made on other phones appear within about 10 seconds, or when you press **Refresh**.

## What the messages mean

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A switch flips back and says it was **not saved** | Usually a dropped connection. Try again. If it names an account, tell the operator: the workbook isn't shared. |
| **No roster yet.** | Tell the operator to press **Update roster**. |
| **Ask the operator for a new desk link.** | Your link has stopped. Ask for a new one. |

A name missing from the list, or written two ways: tell the operator. Don't type a new one.

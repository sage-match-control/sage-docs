    Prize draw (Lucky Dink)¶

    tools/prize-draw.html is one self-contained page: an inline <style> and one <script type="module">. Its rules are the engine module lib/v1/domain/prize-draw.js; the page imports platform.js, data/registry.js, data/snapshot.js, data/api.js, data/operator-session.js and that module, by root-absolute paths, and declares nothing the module exports. Control Center's Open prize draw tile and the tools roster link to it. The same file is the display window, with ?display=1.

    The rule (domain/prize-draw.js)¶

    • Entries. One per line, white space collapsed, the same person (by attendanceKey) dropped. 2 to 1000 entries, each at most 40 characters, with no |.
    • Draw code and id. 8 characters from 23456789ABCDEFGHJKMNPQRSTVWXYZ, from crypto.getRandomValues at Lock; the draw id is made the same way. Nobody types one.
    • Fingerprint. SHA-256, lowercase hex, of the entries joined by \n.
    • Spin n's winners. Each entrant still in gets the key SHA-256(code|n|entry); a spin that draws k winners takes the k smallest keys, smallest first. The prize never enters the hash. Math.random() only fills the reel's cosmetic rows and moves the jar's balls.
    • Prize slots. One per unit, id <row id>:<k>. A spin draws the first slot not yet won, or every unwon slot of its row when the row is Draw all at once. A slot is won by a pick with result won; redrawn frees it.
    • Who is in. A winner drawn again is always out; a prize winner is out under remove-winners, in under keep-winners.
    • The check (verifyDraw) replays every spin and compares winners place by place.

    State¶

    One object in localStorage['sage.prizeDraw'] (version 2): title, event, day, facility, names, rule, showTickets, style, prizeRows ({ id, name, quantity, together }, ids from a counter, rowSeq), then, once locked, drawId, entries, seed, fingerprint, lockedAt, picks (spin, place, slotId, prize, ticket, winner, result, drawnAt, recorded) and parts (earlier draws after Edit list). The display window never reads or writes it.

    The reel and the jar¶

    • Reel. Five rows (64px, 52px on a phone, 96px on the big screen and display), the winner's row third; 46 filler rows, then the winner, over 4.8 s (cubic-bezier(.08,.72,.12,1)); with reduced motion the result shows after 400 ms.
    • Jar. A 640 by 400 canvas, drum centre (190, 190), radius 150, tray at y = 372. Ball radius max(6, min(19, 19·√(40/n))); labels up to 40 balls; the jar is unavailable above 300 names. Gravity .38, damping .996, wall restitution .75, drum spin up to .07 while mixing (2.8 s), then each winner leaves 0.6 s after the last, drops 0.7 s and rolls 1.1 s along the tray. Above 120 balls collisions use a grid.

    The display window¶

    Both windows share BroadcastChannel('sage.prize-draw-display'). The display sends hello, here (every 2 s), played (spin) and bye; the controls send scene (title, event line, style, tickets, rows, entrants, last reveal, next prize), play (spin, style, entrants, picks) and close. A display counts as connected until 5 s pass without hello or here. The results, Draw again and the exports stay on the controls.

    Sign-in and recording¶

    The page asks other tabs of this browser for the operator sign-in over data/operator-session.js (answer: false), keeps it in memory only, and uses it for the people list (GET /v3/days/{day}/facilities/{facility}/people, Load checked in) and for recording.

    Each spin and each Draw again is sent in order, one at a time, to PUT /v3/days/{day}/facilities/{facility}/prize-draws/{drawId}/spins/{spin}; a spin's picks are marked recorded when the answer arrives, so the queue survives a reload. A failure stops the queue (Retry resumes it); a 409 stops it without retry. Spinning never waits. Past draws reads GET …/prize-draws, checks a draw (list against fingerprint, then verifyDraw) and continues one.

    The DRAWS tab¶

    One row per winner, columns A to O: drawId, title, spin, place, prize, ticket, winner, result (won or redrawn), drawnAt (the API's clock), seed, fingerprint, rule, entries, then, on spin 1, place 1 only, list (every entry, one per line in the cell) and continues. Rows are written whole, as typed (RAW), by the API's service account; the API creates the tab on the first spin and refuses another header row (409 draws_layout). src/draws/ holds domain/drawsTab.mjs, domain/drawRequest.mjs, DrawService.mjs and the routes; nothing is published, and the routes take an operator token only. See API.

    Tests¶

    _tests/unit/domain/prize-draw.test.mjs (the rule, with pinned winners) and _tests/unit/tools/prize-draw-page.test.mjs (one module script, no copy of the rules, every named element) in the site repo; test/unit/draws/, test/integration/draw-routes.test.mjs and the SheetsClient tests in the API.
